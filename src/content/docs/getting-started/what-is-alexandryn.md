---
title: What Alexandryn is
description: A self-hosted digital library that runs on your own computer or home server, and what it keeps where.
---

Alexandryn is a digital library that you run yourself. It runs on your desktop, or
on a home server with Docker, and you read your books from any device on your home
network. There is no cloud service and no third-party account.

It keeps three things apart, so that changing one does not disturb the others:

- **Your metadata.** Titles, authors, covers, and editions. Alexandryn looks them
  up on [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org) and stores them on your machine.
- **Your sources.** Where your book files come from: a folder on the server, or
  an OPDS catalog. Sources can change or disappear without erasing your reading
  history.
- **Your library.** What you have read, what you are reading, and how far you have
  got, kept on your own server.

Alexandryn reads your books from where they already are. It does not keep its own
copy of your files, and your reading data can be exported.

## Where to go next

- To use Alexandryn on one computer, [install the desktop app](/docs/getting-started/install-the-desktop-app/).
- To keep it running in the background on a server or NAS, [run it with Docker](/docs/getting-started/run-with-docker/).
